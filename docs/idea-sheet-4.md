# Above the Clouds: idea sheet 4

*Crossed out = built (build sheet 5, 2026-11-12).*

A long menu of ideas, written against the game as it is now (12 biomes up to the Dragon's
Spine, Trail Boons, dares, expeditions, mastery, lifts, Golden City). Nothing here is
agreed. Pick what you like and it goes onto a build sheet.

Each idea has a size: **S** (an evening), **M** (a few sessions), **L** (a milestone of its own).

## 1 · Gameplay

### 1.1 Crux pitches (the boss of every biome) · L
The last 15 m of every biome is a **crux**: one hand-written pitch per biome, the hardest
phrase in it, with its own full arrangement of the biome's motif. The camera pulls in, the
light changes, Bram goes quiet. Clean it and you top out to a big ledge with a view and a
fanfare; slip and you get the normal catch.

- The Stone Wall crux is under a rockfall that falls in rhythm *with* the song.
- The Frozen Reach crux is climbed in a whiteout, by ear only.
- The Clockwork Bastion crux is inside the clock face: the hands sweep across the track.
- The Dragon's Spine crux is the **Dragon's Gate** (see §5).

Crux clears get their own stamp page and a mastery sticker. They also make good Daily Climb
moments, because everyone hits the same crux.

### 1.2 The down arrow · M
Right now you only press ← ↑ →. Adding **↓** (swipe down on touch) opens new moves:
- **Drop**: let go and catch the hold below on the next beat (needed on overhangs and to reach a gem below you).
- **Dyno**: ↓ then ↑ a beat later: crouch and leap. Leaps two holds at once; a miss is a slip.
- **Bounce**: mushroom pads or rope swings that you press ↓ on to spring higher.
Introduce it in one biome (the Glowcap Hollow, §4.3) before it spreads.

### 1.3 Overhangs and roofs · M
Short sections where the wall leans out over you. The camera tilts, the climber hangs under
the rock, notes come in from the top of the track instead of the side, and grip drains a bit
faster. The view under a roof (sky below your feet) is a great screenshot moment.

### 1.4 Traverses and hidden caves · M
Now and then the routes offer a **sideways traverse** (only ← and → notes). It gains no
height but leads to a **cave**: a small dark room with a fossil, a journal page, a gem cache
or a sleeping creature. Risk without reward in height, reward in finds.

### 1.5 Tempo changes inside a climb · S
Notes that speed up (accelerando) or slow down (ritardando) inside one climb. The note
spacing on the track shows it. Fits the Clockwork Bastion (the clock winds down) and the
Storm Crown (the storm gathers).

### 1.6 Call and response · M
A creature or instrument plays a short phrase first (a bird, a horn, a monk's bell), the
track stays empty, then you play it back from memory. Small at first (3 notes), up to 6 high
up. The songbird boon already proves players like hearing the arrows.

### 1.7 Pitons you place yourself · S
New gear: **pitons** (pack of 2). Press ↓ while hanging on a normal hold to hammer one in on
the beat: it becomes a mini checkpoint for this biome only. Hammering costs grip. This gives
"save here" a real decision.

### 1.8 Rescue a stranded climber · M
Rare event: a climber hangs from a broken rope on a route. Take that route and they climb
**behind you**, copying your notes: every miss you make, they make too. Bring them to a
ledge and they give you something (a gem pouch, a card, their flag at base camp forever).
Lose them and they fall back to camp safely, but annoyed.

### 1.9 Bivouac ledges · S
Rarely, a **big ledge** with room for a tent. Camp there and the run fast-forwards to the
next dawn or dusk (your choice), refills you to 120 grip and saves a "camp" you can restart
from once. Pairs nicely with day/night mechanics and Cat's Eyes.

### 1.10 Mountain events (one per run, seeded) · M
A run-wide surprise announced by Bram at the start:
- **Avalanche season**: once per biome a slide comes; climb fast to the next ledge or get swept back.
- **Migration**: thousands of cranes fly past; any perfect hit lets one drop a feather (gems).
- **Eclipse**: for one biome the sun goes out and the Obsidian darkness rules apply.
- **Meteor shower**: shooting stars land as gems on random holds.
- **Rival**: another climber races you up the same seed; beat them to the next ledge for gems.

### 1.11 Weight and greed · S
Every gem you carry this run adds a tiny bit of grip cost (shown as a fuller backpack).
**Cache** your gems at a cairn to bank them. Simple push-your-luck that makes ledges matter
more. Could be a boon or a dare rather than the base rule.

### 1.12 Two-hand lanes (a hard mode) · L
A separate mode, **Duet**: the track splits into a left hand lane and a right hand lane, each
with its own rhythm, like a drum game. Huge skill ceiling, opt-in only.

### 1.13 New boons to add to the cairn
- **METRONOME**: a tick plays on every beat. *Price:* the music is quieter.
- **SHERPA**: once per biome, a missed note is played for you. *Price:* gems worth 30% less.
- **STORMCALLER**: lightning strikes give +10 grip. *Price:* storms in every biome.
- **GOAT HOOVES**: ledges are twice as common. *Price:* ledges only give 60 grip.
- **CROWSONG**: a crow points at the route with a gem. *Price:* it steals one gem per biome.
- **LAST LIGHT** (legendary): when grip hits 0, freeze time for one beat to save it. *Price:* you only get one slip in the whole run.
- Synergy **NIGHT SHIFT**: Cat's Eyes + Stormcaller: storms only come at night, and lightning shows the next three notes.

### 1.14 New dares
- **Silent Mountain**: no music, only the metronome click.
- **Fragile**: every hold crumbles.
- **Pacifist Dragon**: the dragon is awake the whole time but never breathes. It just stares.
- **Tourist**: one route only, always the middle one.
- **Featherweight**: one slip in the whole run instead of per biome.

## 2 · Look, feel and graphics

### 2.1 The rope · M
A rope trails from your harness down to the last anchor (a ledge or a piton): it sags,
swings in the wind, freezes white in the Frozen Reach, glows in the Crystal Heights. On a
fall the rope goes taut and you swing to a stop like a pendulum, with a snap sound. On a
run-ending fall, it snaps. Ropes become a cosmetic slot (red classic, rainbow, braided gold,
dragon sinew).

### 2.2 Music that grows with your streak · M
Each biome's music is split into layers. At 0 streak: just pad and the motif. At 5:
percussion. At 10: bass. At 20: the full lead. Break the streak and it drops back to the bare
layer with a muffled, low-pass "breath" before it builds again. Players will *hear* their
streak and want to keep it.

### 2.3 Biome-skinned HUD · M
The note track and grip meter change with the biome: a wooden plank in the Green Rise,
carved granite on the Stone Wall, a strip of ice that cracks when you miss in the Frozen
Reach, a comb of wax in the Amber Hive, a brass rail with cogs in the Bastion, a bone in the
Bone Garden, a line of embers on the Dragon's Spine. The track frame crossfades as you climb
into a new biome.

### 2.4 Soft biome borders · S
Instead of a hard swap, the last 20 m of a biome blend into the next: snow starts on the
Stone Wall, wax drips into the ice, frost creeps over the glass. Makes the mountain feel like
one place.

### 2.5 Transitions with character · S
Each biome title card arrives with its own wipe: frost growing over the screen, honey
dripping down, gears turning the screen, lightning cracking it, embers burning it away.

### 2.8 Climb timelapse at the end · M
After a run, a 6-second timelapse: the camera pulls back over the whole mountain and your

### 2.9 Light on the climber · S
Rim light from the sun and from biome light sources: orange from the dragon's fire, blue
from the aurora, pink from prism light, a lightning flash that throws a hard shadow on the
wall for one frame.

### 2.10 Things in front of the camera · S
Every so often, something passes **between** you and the climber: a cloud bank that blurs
the screen for a second, a crane flying past huge and close, a drift of snow, a curtain of
bees, a falling ember. Gives depth.

### 2.11 Wall wear · S
Your chalk marks already exist. Add old ones from **other players' runs** (flags data):
faded chalk on holds others used, a dropped glove where someone fell, a carved name on a
ledge. Makes the friends-scale community visible.

## 3 · Replayability

### 3.1 Your ghost · M
A see-through climber plays back your best run on this seed (or your record on the current
mode) beside you, with their chalk line. Overtake them and they wave. Friends' ghosts on
the Daily Climb. Seeded runs make this nearly free: store the inputs, not the positions.

### ~~3.2 Thin Air tiers · M~~ ✓ built
After you pass the Dragon's Gate once, Normal gets **Thin Air 1 to 10**. Each tier adds one
permanent rule on top of the previous ones (tier 1: ledges a bit further apart, tier 2: one
fewer gem per biome, tier 3: the dragon breathes twice from the start...). Each tier has its
own record and a new mark on your flag. This is the classic "ascension" ladder that keeps
the best players busy for months.

### 3.3 Characters · M
Pick who climbs, each with a gift and a price (same design as boons):
- **Elsa**: sees one note further ahead. Her grip drains faster.
- **Young Bram**: starts every run with a free gear item. His beat is 5% faster.
- **The Goat**: double jumps on ledges (skip a ledge). Cannot hold hold-notes well.
- **The Monk**: storms and whiteouts have no effect. No gems from risky routes.
- **The Thief**: gems on every route. Two slips per *run*, not per biome.
- **The Dragon Child** (secret, see §5.K): breathes fire back.
Unlock them through the story, mastery and camp requests.

### 3.4 Mountain builder · L
A small editor: pick biomes in any order, set the tempo, write a few crux pitches, and
share it as a code. Friends climb each other's mountains. The expedition data format
already exists, so the editor writes the same kind of data.

### ~~3.5 Camp requests (small quests) · M~~ ✓ built
A notice board (or people at the fire) with three requests at a time:
- ~~The photographer wants a photo of the dragon **awake**.~~ ✓
- ~~The geologist wants three storm topazes in one run.~~ ✓
- ~~The old woman wants you to find her husband's ice axe (the "Old Ice Axe" discovery).~~ ✓
- ~~A child wants a goat feather (there is no such thing; the joke pays off later).~~ ✓
Each gives gems, a card and sometimes a story scene. They add goals besides height.

### 3.6 Mutated mountains · S
Rare seeds with a twist, announced at the trailhead like the Golden City: **Mirror
Mountain** (the whole mountain flipped), **Two Dragons**, **Night Forever**, **Gem Rain**,
**Upside Order** (the biomes come in reverse). One in 30 seeds. Bram's forecast can hint at
them.

### 3.7 Rush and Zen modes · M
- **Rush**: a timer instead of slips. Ledges add time, perfects add seconds. 5-minute runs.
- **Zen**: no grip, no falls, the music plays fully and you just climb and look. Great for
  new players and for people who want the views.

### 3.8 Altitude titles · S
Every 250 m above 2000 m earns a title on your flag: Cloudwalker, Skyreader, Starhand,
Dragonfriend, Oxygen Thief, The One Bram Talks About.

### 3.9 Biome trials · M
A single biome as a short challenge with a medal: climb the Stone Wall in under 90 s with no
rock hits, climb the Aurora in full swing with no misses. Bronze, silver, gold, plus a
"dev time" medal.

### 3.10 A pet that grows · M
A companion that rides on your pack and grows across runs: a goat kid, a crystal moth, a
bee from the Hive, a baby dragon (§5.K). Each has one small helpful habit and a growth bar
filled by distance climbed.

## 4 · New biomes

Every biome needs one twist, one look, one song and one gem. These fit **between** the
existing ones (the mountain has room for more) or in the high loop above 1950 m.

### 4.1 The Cascade · twist: **Spray**
A gorge with a waterfall falling down the wall beside you. Every few seconds the waterfall
swings across the routes; holds under it are wet: hits cost more grip but every perfect on a
wet hold fills the "rainbow" meter, and a full rainbow gives a free climb.
*Look:* mist, rainbows, ferns, slick black rock. *Song:* kalimba and rain. *Gem:* river pearl.

### 4.2 The Hanging Monastery · twist: **Bells**
Temples built into the cliff, wooden walkways, prayer wheels. Monks ring a bell before some
climbs: the bell plays the phrase, then you echo it with a blank track (call and response,
§1.6). Ledges are temple balconies where a monk pours tea (+grip).
*Look:* red wood, golden roofs, prayer flags, incense smoke. *Song:* singing bowls and
throat chant. *Gem:* jade.

### 4.3 The Glowcap Hollow · twist: **Bounce**
The climb goes *inside* the mountain through giant glowing mushrooms. Mushroom caps are
pads: press ↓ on the beat to bounce up two holds (introduces the down arrow, §1.2). Spores
drift in front of the track and glow on the beat.
*Look:* deep blue and violet, glowing caps, fireflies. *Song:* soft synth, bubbly plucks.
*Gem:* moonstone.

### 4.4 The Hoodoo Canyon · twist: **Heat shimmer**
Red sandstone pillars in the desert wind. Heat makes the notes wobble on the track, so you
play by ear more than by eye; the beat itself never changes. A sandstorm now and then hides
the backdrop.
*Look:* terracotta, cacti on ledges, vultures, a giant sun. *Song:* slide guitar and hand
drum. *Gem:* fire agate.

### 4.5 The Glacier Heart · twist: **Melt**
You climb inside a blue ice cave in the glacier. As the day warms, holds melt: each hold
has a drip timer and is gone after a few beats. Climb fast in the afternoon, slow at night.
*Look:* see-through blue ice with old things frozen inside (a mammoth, a sword, a plane).
*Song:* glass harmonica. *Gem:* blue ice crystal.

### 4.6 The Moth Wall · twist: **Swarm of light**
A night biome. Giant soft moths sleep on the holds. Each perfect hit flashes your headlamp
and scatters the moths from the next hold; a miss leaves them there and the hold is
"furry" (costs more grip). At dawn they all fly off at once (a gorgeous moment).
*Look:* dusky purple, pale moths, paper lanterns. *Song:* celesta lullaby. *Gem:* moth-wing
opal.

### 4.7 The Wind Organ · twist: **Chords**
Old stone pipes built into the cliff by someone long gone. Wind plays them. Hold notes here
are chords: hold the arrow and the organ plays, release on the beat to end the chord
cleanly. Long holds get longer.
*Look:* white marble pipes, ivy, banners. *Song:* the organ itself is the music. *Gem:*
pearl of wind.

### 4.8 The Giant's Stair · twist: **Breathing wall**
Huge stone steps carved for something enormous. A stone giant sleeps against the cliff.
Its slow breathing tilts the whole wall a little in and out, and the tempo follows: a bit
faster on the in-breath, slower out. Don't wake him (a crux pitch where he does).
*Look:* moss-green, colossal carvings, tiny shrines on the steps. *Song:* low brass, slow
boom drum. *Gem:* giant's tear (a huge, cheap one, funny).

### 4.9 The Lantern Bazaar · a rest biome, no hazard
A market hanging off the cliff on ropes: stalls, lanterns, cats. No twist: the music is
cheerful, the climbs are easy and there's a **mid-run trader** who sells gear for this run
only. A breather between two hard biomes.

### 4.10 Seasonal biomes (replace one biome for a few weeks)
- **Hollow Peak** (Halloween): pumpkins on holds, ghost climbers that copy you one beat
  late, a bat swarm instead of the bees.
- **The Lantern Pass** (Christmas): snow globes, gift boxes on holds (gems), a sleigh in the
  sky, jingle percussion layered on every biome song.
- **Bloom Ridge** (spring): cherry blossom petals blow across the track on the beat.

## 5 · After the dragon

Right now the high biomes loop forever after 1950 m. Here are many ideas for what could be
up there, then a recommended way to stitch the best ones together.

### A · The Dragon's Gate (the boss)
At the top of the Dragon's Spine the dragon wakes for good and **does not go back to
sleep**. A long crux pitch: it breathes on every fourth climb, its wings make gusts, the
music is a full battle version of the throat-singing motif. At the top, the dragon rears,
looks at you... and moves aside. You've earned the way. (Or it flies off and the second
time you meet it, it takes you somewhere, see C.)

### B · Riding the dragon
The dragon takes off with you clinging to its back. The "wall" is now its scaly neck, the
screen rolls as it banks, wingbeats are the beat, and you climb scale by scale toward its
head while the world turns below. Ends when it drops you at the next place. A one-off
setpiece every run that passes the Gate.

### C · The Heavens (a new band of biomes)
Above the dragon the sky turns dark blue in the daytime. Stars come out. You're climbing
into space.
- **The Thin Blue**: air is thin, a breath meter fills while you hang and drains on hits; ledges are oxygen pockets.
- **The Moon Steps**: rocks float up toward the moon. Low gravity: notes float slower, and every leap overshoots (one extra hold, sometimes too far).
- **The Star Loom**: threads of light between stars. Notes are stars; hit them to weave a constellation, complete one for a gem cache.
- **The Comet's Tail**: the last stretch is a comet sweeping past. You climb its tail of ice and fire while it moves.

### D · The Dragon's Hoard
Behind the dragon, a cave full of gold. Gems on every hold, but each gem makes your pack
heavier and the dragon's sleep lighter. Leave when you want. Pure push-your-luck, a perfect
"one more" moment.

### E · Titan's Brow
The twist: the mountain is a giant, and the dragon was its guardian. Above the Spine you
climb its face: the brow, the closed eye (which opens), the stone hair. Its breath is the
tempo. Its waking is the true final boss.

### F · The Mirror Summit
At the very top the mountain continues *upside down* into the sky: a reflection of the
whole climb. All biomes again, in reverse order, mirrored, at night. The upside-down
mountain is the endgame loop, so the "endless" part is earned and has its own record.

### G · The Sky City
The Golden City was never an accident. Above the dragon is where it comes from: a real
city in the clouds, abandoned. Climb its towers, ring its bells. The secret 1-in-200 city
becomes a teaser of the real thing.

### H · The Roc's Eyrie and the Far Range
A giant bird's nest at the top. The roc carries you to **another mountain** across the
cloud sea, with a new set of biomes (a desert mountain, a jungle one, an ocean sea-stack).
A second world without throwing away the first.

### I · The Clock That Stopped
Time freezes near the summit (connects to the Clockwork Bastion). No music: the beat comes
from *your own* first three taps. Climb at whatever tempo you set. Strange and beautiful;
best as a short one-off moment.

### J · Elsa's summit
The story ending. Bram and Elsa never finished their climb. At the top, Elsa's flag, half
torn, and her last journal page. You plant your flag next to hers. Credits roll over the
cloud sea at dawn with every biome's motif as one song. Back at camp, Bram has something
new to say for the rest of the game.

### K · The Dragon's Egg
The dragon was guarding an egg. At the top you find it. Bring it down (a run back to camp
where the egg must not be dropped: slips crack it). At camp it hatches: a baby dragon pet
(§3.10) that grows over many runs and finally becomes a playable character (§3.3) that
breathes fire *back* at the next dragon.

### L · The Weather Maker
Above the Storm Crown and the dragon is the place weather is made: a machine or a god.
Every biome's weather (whiteouts, storms, aurora, thermals) comes back at once and you
choose which to switch off as you climb, one per ledge. The fewer you switch off, the more
gems.

### M · Back to the ground
A wild one: the top of the mountain is the **bottom** of another, upside-down world. You
come out of the clouds at the Green Rise of a different valley, at night, and everything is
a little wrong. A short, eerie epilogue biome.

### Recommended stitch: "Act II"

1. **The Dragon's Gate** (A) is the first real finish line, around 1950 m. Passing it once
   unlocks everything below.
2. **Riding the dragon** (B) carries you over the gap.
3. **Act II: The Heavens** (C): four new biomes from about 2000 m to 3000 m: The Thin Blue,
   The Moon Steps, The Star Loom, The Comet's Tail. Each with a twist, song and gem, using
   the same biome file format.
4. **Elsa's summit** (J) at 3000 m: the story ending and credits, plus the **egg** (K).
5. After that, the **Mirror Summit** (F) as the endless loop with its own record, and the
   **Thin Air tiers** (§3.2) unlock for Normal.

That gives the game a beginning, a boss, an ending and an endgame, which it doesn't have
yet. The Hoard (D) and the Sky City (G) can live in Act II as rare secret rooms.
